Spec work. No brief from L'Oréal, no relationship with L'Oréal, public information only.

When you think hair, you think L'Oréal. Bald people almost certainly do not — and they are not one audience either. For some it was a choice, for others a decision taken for them, and the category treats those two completely different emotional starting points as one demographic, when it addresses them at all.

The scalp does not disappear when the hair does. It still burns, still dries, still ages. The biological need survives perfectly intact while the marketing stops speaking, which makes the exclusion a positioning decision rather than a product one.
