Spec work. Vastr does not exist. Every part of it was built from nothing, including the parts I threw away.

A full wardrobe and nothing to wear is not a joke about shopping, it is a memory problem. People who love clothes accumulate faster than they can recall, and the existing apps answer that by asking you to photograph and tag everything first — a second job, handed to the people least likely to finish it.

The primary message came out as: *you already own the outfit, Vastr just finds it.*
