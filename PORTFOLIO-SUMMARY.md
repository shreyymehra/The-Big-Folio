# PORTFOLIO SUMMARY

What is on each tab, in what words, with what device, and what state it is in.
Written for you to read top to bottom once and verify, not for the repo.

Audited against the running site, 7 Oct 2026.

## THE SYSTEM, IN ONE PAGE

**What the site is.** Nine routes of hand-written HTML, one shared stylesheet,
no framework and no build step. Tailwind compiles the five case pages only, and
its output is committed, so what deploys is what is in the repo.

**Ground.** Off-white `#F7F4EC` with a hand-drawn grid printed into it: 90px
cells, lines that wobble between crossings, pinned at the tile edge so it repeats
without a seam. The grid is drawn from your own reference image, held at 30%
strength on light and 18% on dark, which is the point where body copy over it
reads exactly as it does on blank paper.

**Colour.** Superman, aged to newsprint. Cape blue `#173CA8` carries every line
of type and every rule. Cape red `#E8251F` carries planes and type above 24px,
with `#B8141A` for red at small sizes. Shield yellow `#FFED10` is an accent only:
a highlighter, a star, a hover. There is no fourth colour and no grey.

**Type.** Asimov Print B for every heading, with a pressed-ink edge so the
letterforms bleed the way a cheap press bleeds. Inter for body. Geist Light for
annotation and metadata. Nothing is pure black anywhere on the site.

**Rules the whole thing obeys.** No rounded cards, no shadows, no pills, no
badges, no gradients, no centred layouts, no em dashes in the copy. Every section
gets its own device so no two read the same. Every claim that is spec says so in
its first line. Every number on the site is from your resume, and where a number
would help but does not exist, the page shows the reasoning instead.

**Accessibility.** Contrast passes AA on every route in both themes. Reduced
motion stops every loop rather than just shortening it. Nothing is smaller than
24px as a target. The page reflows at 390px instead of shrinking. There is a 404.

## HOME

Eight screens, in this order: hero, quote tape, logo wall, one ticker, work,
ideas, me, contact.

### Hero

Your face is the centrepiece and everything else orbits it. The photograph sits
in the middle with the ransom-note name beside it, and six labelled marks ride a
rectangular path around the outside, upright the whole way like horses on a
carousel. The ring clears the centre rather than passing over or behind it, so
every mark stays clickable and nothing crosses the copy.

Copy, verbatim:

- Name, cut-out tiles: **Hi my name is Shrey**
- Claim: **I build brands people want to belong to.**
- Rolling line: **and I have always wanted to build** [brands / experiences /
  campaigns / launches / communities / worlds]
- Discipline: **Product marketing · GTM · Brand strategy**
- Two calls to action on one line: **See the work** and the email address
- Photo caption: **Delhi, 2003. First machine I was allowed to break.**

The photograph is black and white, and the pointer paints colour back into it as
it moves, which is the one device on the site that rewards a visitor for doing
nothing but being there. It is running on the wrong source file; see item A on
the fill-in list.

The six marks: Work, Ideas, Me, FAQ, Contact, Listening. The last one starts the
record player, which stays out of the way while the hero holds the screen.

## THE FIVE CASE PAGES

All five run the same shape, so a reader who has read one knows where to look in
the next. Each opens with your poster framed whole, capped so it cannot push the
body off the first screen.

**The spine of every page**

1. **Role and constraints** — Role, Brief, Constraint, Made or Shipped or State.
   This is the first thing a hiring manager wants and it used to be buried.
2. **The narrative** — problem, hypothesis, the work, the result, the learning.
3. **Key decisions** — three per page, each with Options, Why, and a Trade-off on
   a red rule. The trade-off is what separates a case study from a brochure. Your
   reasoning, re-cut; the trade-off lines are the ones written for you and are the
   sentences most worth reading with a pen.
4. **A reading rail** at the top of the window, and a contents list that follows
   where you are.

**Vastr** (Spec). The only piece that starts from a blank page, so it carries the
world-building alone: the problem, the name and the two names that lost (Debra,
Whear), three features, the brand, the messaging, the launch, a one-page GTM
brief, and an explicit "no numbers, because there are none". Decisions: the name;
affiliate that fires on a purchase decision rather than on time spent;
recognition rather than data entry.

**Cut The Noise** (Spec). "Hungry Jack's is not losing to McDonald's. It's losing
to the feed." The argument is restraint as a campaign. Decisions: leave the feed
instead of competing in it; spend the heritage the brand actually has; live
capture, no actors. The Burger King correction holds: the 1985 store is Hungry
Jack's own heritage and the page never implies otherwise.

**VoiceDNA** (In build). The only page that is a screen rather than paper: ink
ground, warm type, coral accent, and it flips the other way in dark mode, so the
page is always the opposite of wherever the visitor is. It carries the gap map at
the size it is read: two sample analyses behind a tablist, six dimensions, stated
versus actual with the gap band between them. Both samples are fictional brands
and are labelled as an illustration on the page itself. Never called live
anywhere on the site.

**Venus Roadlines** (Shipped). The only real, shipped piece. Decisions: bilingual
by design rather than a translation layer; structured for the reader rather than
the organisation; an annexure system instead of one document. Result: three out of
four across three audits. **Its tag is the one open contradiction on the site;**
see Decisions in the fill-in list.

**AbsolutILY** (Spec). "For the wins that had no witnesses." Five executions, all
unwitnessed wins, which is also the compliance position: the alcohol code
prohibits positioning a drink as a response to adversity, so no execution sits on
a bad moment. The page says the constraint improved the work, which is true and
is the most hireable sentence on it.

## WHAT I WOULD FIX NEXT, IN ORDER

1. **Put names on the six quotes.** Six anonymous compliments under the hero is
   the single weakest thing on the site, and it is twenty minutes of your time.
2. **The colour photograph.** The best interaction on the site is running at half
   strength for want of one file.
3. **One real Ideas post.** The section is in the navigation and promises a point
   of view. One piece converts the promise into evidence; until then it is the
   only place the site asks to be believed rather than showing something.
4. **Settle the Venus Roadlines tag.** It is the only internal contradiction left,
   and it sits on the one piece that is genuinely real, which is the worst place
   for it.
5. **Build the Me deck** once the recommendations land.

## WHAT I NOTICED THAT YOU HAVE NOT ASKED ABOUT

- **The home h1 is five images.** Screen readers get "Hi my name is Shrey"
  correctly from the alt text. Search engines read alt text too, but weight it
  less than real text, so the strongest headline on the site is the one Google
  understands least. Worth knowing; not worth undoing the device for.
- **The section numbers disagreed across pages.** Home called Me 04 and Contact
  05, while /about/ said 03 and /contact/ said 06. Fixed this pass: the numbers
  now run 02 Work, 03 Ideas, 04 Me, 05 Contact everywhere.
- **A typo in a project title.** "The Bird That Flew Too Close the Sun" was
  missing a word. Fixed on both pages it appears on.
- **The Me figure slot collapsed below 900px.** It is a `<span>`, and inline
  elements ignore width and height, so the square slot flattened into a 62px band
  across the column on every phone and small laptop. Fixed this pass.
- **The resume is a .docx.** It downloads, but it opens in an editor rather than
  a reader and reflows on someone else's machine. PDF is the format a recruiter
  expects (G17).
- **Both deploy targets are live.** Two URLs, two branches, and nothing says
  which is canonical.
