02 / Brain dump Five steps. Same order, every time.

Brief in, work out. This is the bit between.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Undoubtedly the first step — listen to what they want. Knowing what to do and how to do it is 80% of the result, so pay attention to what the goal actually is and note everything in detail. As if you are taking notes. Really.

  2. 02

    Blank canvas

    Make no associations. Approach the task like a stranger who has heard none of the relevant details, because that is what lets me bring a perspective from scratch instead of arriving with the opinion already formed.

  3. 03

    Read, research & relay

    Sharpening the axe is the fastest way to cut down the tree. Research is where I spend the most time — it keeps the work fresh, mistake-proof-ish and backed by something. It doubles as a knowledge bank, so this step always carries forward.

  4. 04

    Time for tango

    Final checks, from grammar to spelling. Always present a variation or two — not to confuse, but so there is plenty to choose from. Keep the logic behind every choice ready to hand, because that is what makes the decision easy for the person on the other side.

  5. 05

    Rinse & repeat

    Apply it across copies, ads, designs, brief decks, whatever is being made. Make it the standard of the work, and the work stands out from the standard.

I like to connect with people. My high-school crush called me an ‘empath’ once and I took it seriously as a job title. I am a creative marketer who builds, a communicator who listens, and most importantly a human who knows how to blend words with numbers.

My domain has always been brand, communications, creative and public relations — anything that makes a brand perform, not performative. Transparency, authenticity, trust and loyalty drive this new generation of brand building.

Here is what my resume may or may not tell you: I bring cultural fluency from a multicultural upbringing, blended with a knack for pop culture (debate me, anytime), fashion, tech and marketing.
