The Ochre problem

Brain Dump is assigned Ochre as its section ink, on a Cream ground with a 4% Ochre wash — which makes the ground warmer, pushing the pair below 1.92. The FAQ hand-drawn numbers are Ochre on Cream tiles. Both are unreadable as written.

Don't drop Ochre. Invert it.

Ochre fails as ink and passes as paper. Deep type on an Ochre fill measures 5.49 — comfortably above the body threshold. The section keeps its warmth, the numbers stay legible, and the material logic actually improves: a riso ochre plate reads as a printed block, not as coloured text.
