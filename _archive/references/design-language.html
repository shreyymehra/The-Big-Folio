Every adjacent word is a different typeface. The mismatch is the effect. Nothing below is decoration — each piece is a rule you can hand to a build session and get the same result twice. Interactions are live: hover, click, scroll.

01

Palette

Near-black ground, paper labels, three loud accents. One accent per viewport, never two. The pink and the blue never touch — put paper or void between them or the whole thing turns into a sticker bomb.

Void#0b0b0dall grounds

Cold blue#2f5bffsecondary · states

02

The five cuts

Five families, no more. Never two adjacent words on the same cut. Assign by feel, not by rule — but once a headline is set, it is set; re-rolling on every load makes the site feel unfinished rather than alive.

cut=1
Playfair Display Italic
the romantic · abstractions, feelings, the soft word in a hard line
cut=2
Archivo Black
the shout · verbs, the load-bearing noun, section headers
cut=3
Courier Prime
the evidence · meta, dates, labels, anything that reads as a receipt
cut=4
DM Sans Bold
the neutral · connective tissue, body, the word you don't want noticed
cut=5
BEBAS NEUE
the poster · proper nouns, brand names, anything set in caps

CutWord — the primitive

This matches src/components/CutWord.astro as built. Props: cut 1–5 · tone paper/hot/cold/acid/ink · rotate degrees, keep to ±1–3 · size lg/sm · depth parallax, 0 = pinned. Colours are --hot / --cold / --acid in tokens.css — not pink/blue.

04

Punctuation & furniture

The cut-out look collapses into a poster if type is all it has. These are the non-type marks — lifted straight from the collage references, translated into rules. Starbursts are punctuation, not decoration: they end a thought, they never sit alone.

Starburst · terminal punctuation, ticker separator, "real ✓" stamp
Barcode · case-study index numbers, footer serial. Never scannable — it is a mark, not a claim

05

Layout

Compose with CSS Grid template areas. Never percentage translate3d offsets — percentages resolve against the element's own width, not the viewport, which is how a previous build ended up mobile-shaped and stranded in the middle of a desktop screen. Re-lay-out at 1080px and 760px. Spacing scale as it exists in tokens.css: --s-1 .5rem · --s-2 1rem · --s-3 1.75rem · --s-4 3rem · --s-5 5rem · --s-6 8rem. Nothing between.

a — headline
b — photo
c — meta
d — label
e — ticker

06

Motion lab

nine ideas · all live

--ease-out is cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). Durations are --d-fast 240ms · --d-mid 560ms · --d-slow 900ms. Children stagger 80ms. Banned: bounce, spring overshoot, confetti, parallax on text — with one documented exception: --ease-snap overshoots at 1.06 and exists for the loader's reel settle, because a slot machine physically overshoots. Scope it to Loader.astro. Anywhere else it is drift.

prefers-reduced-motion collapses the duration tokens to 1ms in tokens.css — but that does not stop an infinite animation. The ticker and the reel loop must be paused explicitly in their own components.

07

The floor

Not features. If one of these fails, the section is not done.

CheckRule
FocusVisible on every interactive element — 2px acid outline, 3px offset. Never outline:none without a replacement.
KeyboardWork rows, FAQ tiles, ticker pause and vinyl are all reachable and operable by tab + enter.
Motionprefers-reduced-motion kills every animation on this page, including the ticker and the loader.
ContrastInk on paper and paper on void both clear AA. Acid on paper does not — acid only ever sits on void or ink.
Targets44px minimum. The work row is tall enough; the FAQ tile is padded to reach it.
ReflowContent restacks at 760px, never scales down. Test at 390px.
