Spec: a made-up company, built to show the thinking

Brand
World

A complete brand universe for a made-up AI company. Name, voice, identity, world. Built from a blank page so you can watch every decision get made.

Lead: Brand Support: Creative Strategy 2026 5 min read
01 / Problem

Every AI company is selling the same future.

Capability claims, cosmic gradients, the word intelligence doing all the work. The category talks about what the technology can do and almost never about who you become by using it. So there's nothing to belong to. People join tribes, not feature lists.

The brief I set myself: build a full brand world for a fictional AI company, blank page to finished system, and be ready to defend every choice in an interview.

Paste artwork 01 — category audit
02 / Hypothesis

People adopt what their people adopt.

The category sells capability, so the gap is belonging. Build the brand like a world. Put one human tension at the centre and let everything grow from it: the name, the voice, the look, and the list of things the brand refuses to do.

03 / Approach

One tension, then everything in its orbit.

  • i.Pick the tension. The human ache this product actually answers. No visuals allowed yet.
  • ii.Name it. The routes I tried, the one that survived, and why the others died.
  • iii.Write the voice. What it always says. What it never says.
  • iv.Build the system. Identity, type, colour, and artifacts this world would really produce.
  • v.Prove it. A manifesto, a launch film script, product copy written for real.
Paste artwork 02 — identity system
04 / Result

What exists now.

A named company with a voice, an identity system, a manifesto and a launch script. Nothing shipped to market, and no numbers pretending otherwise. What the piece proves is simpler: I can start with nothing and end with a world that survives questioning.

Paste artwork 03 — world artifacts
05 / Learning

Worlds don't come from mood boards. They come from one tension held long enough to organise everything else.
