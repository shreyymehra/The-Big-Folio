# DIRECTION — governing rules for the build
Supersedes earlier design notes. Written from Shrey's brief, 26 Aug 2026.

## POSITIONING
Culturally fluent, strategically and commercially aware. Territory is marketing, product, GTM,
business ops, creative execution, and AI-enabled ways of working. Not "a designer". Not "a copywriter".

Impression to earn: curious, creative, human, contemporary.
Test for any page: does a visitor understand in 8 seconds whether this person is worth their time.

## THE FIVE JOBS
| Section | Job | Failure mode to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Identity, what I do, personality, curiosity, route to work | A tagline with nothing behind it |
| Work | Primary evidence of capability | An image gallery |
| Ideas | The intellectual layer. POV, observations, editorial | A blog with three posts |
| About | The human context that makes the work memorable | A restated CV |
| Contact | Frictionless, confident | Over-designed |

## HIERARCHY — apply to every block
1. **Idea** — what the visitor must understand. Largest.
2. **Evidence** — what proves it.
3. **Context** — what helps them understand it. Neutral tone, not black.
4. **Metadata** — role, date, tools, category. Smallest, mono, muted.

Never give these equal visual weight. If everything is the same size, nothing has been decided.

## GRID
12 columns desktop, 8 tablet, 4 mobile. Consistent margin and gutter. Strong alignment.
Asymmetric by default. Centred layouts are a deliberate exception, never a fallback.

## TYPE
Carries the identity. Wide gaps between sizes, no intermediate steps to smooth the scale.
Avoid excessive bolding: weight 500 does the work, 700 is a rare emphasis.

## COLOUR
Primary dark, primary light, three neutrals, one accent.
The accent appears a countable number of times per page. If you cannot count them, there are too many.

## MOTION
Must communicate hierarchy, interaction, or transition. Motion that only demonstrates capability is cut.
Reduced motion is respected without going fully static.

## CASE STUDY SHAPE
Context, challenge, thinking, execution, outcome, reflection. Not every stage appears in every piece,
but the narrative must be continuous. Prioritise: my role, the problem, my contribution, the strategic
thinking, the evidence, the result.

Show how I think, not what I made. A beautiful artefact without reasoning is weaker than an
ordinary artefact with strong reasoning.

Visual rhythm across the page: intro, immersion, thinking, evidence, conclusion.
Mix large visuals, detail crops, typography, screenshots, process material, whitespace, explanation.

## VETO — do not ship these
Glassmorphism. Rounded cards. Drop shadows and offset shadows. Pills. Badges.
Corporate copy. Clutter. Decorative scroll lines and section breaks that carry no meaning.
Metadata without context, e.g. a floating "MELBOURNE - GLOBAL" chip.
Em dashes in body copy. Centred symmetrical layouts. Volume for its own sake.

Anything on this list reads as AI-generated, which is the one impression that undoes the rest.

## SIGNAL OVER VOLUME
Short paragraphs. Strong headlines. Pull quotes. Captions. Progressive disclosure.
Having the content is not a reason to show all of it at once.

## REFERENCES
Extract principles: typographic behaviour, composition, rhythm, spatial relationships, interaction
philosophy, tone. Never lift distinctive components, exact layouts, or signature interactions.
The result must read as Shrey's system, informed by references rather than derived from them.

## CREDIBILITY
Flag every gap. Fabricate nothing. Spec work is disclosed in the first line.
No number appears that cannot be evidenced.

## WHEN TWO OPTIONS COMPETE
Choose the more intentional, more distinctive, more useful, more tone-aligned, more restrained one.

## NORTH STAR
A thoughtfully art-directed personal publication by someone who understands culture, marketing and
technology. Taste without pretense. Intelligence without jargon. Creativity without gimmicks.
Ambition and curiosity without self-importance.

The goal is not to impress with what the website can do. It is to make them want to know what Shrey can do.
